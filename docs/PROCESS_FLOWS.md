# Key Process Flows

## A. Record material waste
1. Open Waste Tracker.
2. Search/filter records if needed.
3. Select Add Record.
4. Enter material, category, quantity/unit, date, disposal cost, treatment and notes.
5. Validate required fields and numeric ranges.
6. Save and confirm the record appears.
7. Verify related dashboard/report metrics update.
8. Record test evidence and log defects.

## B. Evaluate treatment costs
1. Open Savings Calculator.
2. Select a category or custom scenario.
3. Enter quantity and cost assumptions.
4. Review formulas and scenario outputs.
5. Independently calculate a small example.
6. Check scenario options use comparable net-cost assumptions.
7. Record mismatches as defects.

## C. List or find surplus materials
1. Open Marketplace.
2. Search/filter listings.
3. Review quantity, unit, condition, location and price.
4. Enter required details to create a listing.
5. Verify demo listings and simulated connections are clearly labelled.
6. Do not represent simulated Connect actions as real transactions.

## D. Review/export reports
1. Open Reports.
2. Select monthly or yearly view.
3. Review KPIs and trends.
4. Export records, summary or trends.
5. Inspect CSV headers and reporting period.
6. Compare with dashboard/source records.
